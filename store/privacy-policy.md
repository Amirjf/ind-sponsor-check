# Privacy Policy — IND Sponsor Check for LinkedIn

_Last updated: 16 September 2026_

**IND Sponsor Check for LinkedIn** (the "Extension") is a browser extension
that shows whether a company shown on LinkedIn or Indeed appears in the public
register of recognised sponsors published by the Dutch Immigration and
Naturalisation Service (IND).

## What the Extension does with data

- The Extension reads the **company name** shown on the LinkedIn or Indeed
  job or company page you are currently viewing. This happens entirely inside your browser.
- The company name is compared against a copy of the IND public register that
  is stored locally in your browser.
- The Extension **does not send the company name, the job you are viewing, or
  any other information about you** to the developer or to any third party.

## Network requests the Extension makes

- **ind.nl**: to download the public register of recognised sponsors
  (approximately once per day).
- **Supabase (a hosted database used by the developer)**: to read a small
  configuration table containing the address of the register page and the
  refresh interval. This request contains no personal data.

No analytics, tracking, advertising or telemetry of any kind is used.

## Data storage

The downloaded register and configuration are stored in your browser's local
extension storage. They are removed when you uninstall the Extension.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at the same URL
